## 6. Mechanism Analysis

### 6.1 Floor Growth Mechanics

1. **Fee Accumulation**: Buy/sell fees → floor elevation
2. **Tier Merging (Safe-Merge)**: Premium tiers absorbed when: `L_f - D >= P_next × (S_0 + M_next) × (1 + buffer)`

### 6.2 Why Presale Outperforms

- Entry at floor price → no premium at risk
- Floor only increases → guaranteed minimum exit
- LST faces depeg risk → additional downside

### 6.3 Why Premium Entry Underperforms (Bear Market)

- Entry at 2x floor → 50% premium at risk
- Floor growth insufficient to offset underlying drop
- Combined loss exceeds LST's loss
